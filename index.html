01 — Who we are

A single trusted partner for everything you can't do from abroad

Living overseas comes with a long list of things that still need attention back home — paperwork, your children's education, and above all, ageing parents who need care. NRI Gateway is the portal built to handle exactly those tasks, in India and the USA, on your behalf.

We act as your eyes, hands and heart on the ground — coordinating with hospitals, schools and government offices so you can stay focused on your life abroad while knowing everything at home is in dependable hands.

  • Verified, background-checked professionals
  • Transparent pricing & competitive rates
  • Quick turnaround with real-time updates
  • One point of contact for every request

“We do truly care about your family.”

That isn't a slogan — it's the standard we hold every service to. From a routine health check for your parents to a critical document attestation, we treat your requests as if they were our own.

— Team NRI Gateway
