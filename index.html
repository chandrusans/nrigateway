01 — Who we are

A single trusted partner for everything you can't do from abroad

NRI Gateway is a service provider for the NRI community. We are committed to bridging the gap between Non-Resident Indians and their roots. Leveraging decades of expertise, we have tailored a spectrum of services that cater to the diverse needs of the global Indian community.

Our offerings span property, financial and legal assistance, immigration, parental care, education and personalized concierge services — all designed to make the overseas experience seamless and connected. Understanding the challenges faced by NRIs, we employ a network of seasoned professionals to deliver solutions that are both efficient and empathetic.

In a world where geographical boundaries are becoming irrelevant, NRI Gateway stands as a trusted partner, succeeding in forging real human connections. Your needs, our solutions.

  • A network of seasoned, verified professionals
  • Efficient and empathetic, every step of the way
  • Real-time updates with a single point of contact
  • Your pathway to peace of mind and prosperity

“We do truly care about your family.”

That isn't a slogan — it's the standard we hold every service to. From a routine health check for your parents to a critical document attestation, we treat your requests as if they were our own.

— Team NRI Gateway
